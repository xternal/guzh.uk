Ruminator

What Russia's regions are really told, beyond Moscow's national channels.

Every day it reads Russia's regional and local press across dozens of regions, from state television to independent and exiled outlets and residents' Telegram channels, and shows what each kind of outlet reports and how it frames it. Every claim is checked word for word against its source. Built for journalists, analysts, researchers, diplomats and investors.

Why it matters in defence: understanding an adversary includes understanding what its own people are being told, region by region, and how that changes.

Questions

Who is this for?

UK defence and dual-use teams of 15 to a few hundred people: drones, counter-drone, autonomy, defence AI, space, advanced manufacturing. Usually just after a raise or a first contract, when the company has to grow up around the product.

What about classified work?

My work sits on the unclassified side: the company around the technology. Where anything needs vetting, we say so plainly at the start, and the cleared work stays with your cleared people.

Is this consulting?

No slide decks. I work inside the team on one bottleneck at a time and build the fix: the production tracker, the supplier process, the bid pack, the hiring pipeline, the internal tool.

The defence know-how is yours, and it stays yours. What I bring is twenty years of building companies and the systems they run on, including national payment infrastructure, where the technology is hard and the stakes are national.
