Questions

Before you book.

Why someone from outside defence?

Because the problems after a raise are the same in every fast-growing company: too much to do, too few people, and processes built for ten staff carrying a hundred. Defence adds rules on top; it doesn't change the arithmetic. The defence judgement stays with you.

Is this a consultancy?

No decks. Working software and processes that run differently the following week, built hands-on with your team.

Do you need a security clearance?

Not for the unclassified work. Where a task needs vetting, we agree at the start who does it and how.

What size of company is this for?

UK defence and dual-use companies from about 15 to a few hundred people: drones, counter-drone, autonomy, defence AI, space, advanced manufacturing. Usually just after a funding round, a first big contract or a new site.

What are Ruminator and LedgerGov.UK, and why mention them?

Projects he builds himself, to the side of his main work, for society and the state: one reads Russia's regional press, the other is an open P&L of the UK state. They show the code is his own, and the kind of problem he cares about.
