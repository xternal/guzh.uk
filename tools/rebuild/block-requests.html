Residents' requests, triaged in seconds

Your team stops reading, sorting and forwarding every resident email by hand. Each one is routed and answered as it arrives, emergencies first, so the same team can run more blocks.

A block manager's shared inbox on a Monday morning. Press the button: each email is sorted by kind and urgency, matched to its block and flat, routed to the right contractor or team, and answered. Chasers are linked to the job already open, so nobody books the same plumber twice.

Invented sample data. In this demo the sorting runs on plain rules in your browser and nothing leaves the page. The production build reads the real inbox, uses a language model with your block list and contractor rota, and a person approves anything it is unsure of.
