A loan pack to a draft credit memo

Your underwriters stop keying bank statements and start every application from a drafted memo with the checks already done. When the facility grows, the same team decides more loans.

When a new facility lifts origination, the bottleneck moves to document intake and affordability review. Pick an applicant and press the button: the application and three months of bank statements are read, checked against a lending policy, and written up as a draft memo. The underwriter starts from the decision, not the data entry.
