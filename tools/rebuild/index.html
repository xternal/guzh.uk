Questions

Why one process, not a transformation programme?
Because one process shipped and measured teaches more than a roadmap. If it works, the next one is cheaper.
What does "measured before and after" mean?
We count the hours and errors in the process before we touch it, and count them again once the new version is live, on the same basis.
Do you replace our systems?
No. The rebuilt process sits on top of the systems you have, through their APIs, exports or inboxes.
What about data protection?
Every build comes with a DPIA and keeps personal data to what the process needs. Where it is hosted is your choice.
