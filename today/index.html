Always Today

What am I doing today?

A calm to-do list that answers that one question every morning. Your lists live on your device and work offline, and they sync across your phone, your iPad, your Googlebook, your Windows PC, your Mac and your Linux terminal when you want them to.

Type a to-do the way you would say it, “call bank tomorrow 3pm #admin”, and it lands in the right list with its day, time and tag. Today, Upcoming, Anytime, Someday and a Logbook; areas and projects with headings; repeats that never double up; reminders; and a quiet line under Today that marks each thing as you finish it. Optional end-to-end encryption, and no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your lists live on your device

Questions

Is Always Today free?

Yes, on one device, for ever: every list, quick entry, repeats, reminders, import and export. You pay only if you want your lists to sync between devices: £24 a year, £3 a month, or £59 once for Lifetime. Every new account gets 7 days of sync free, with no card needed.

How do I buy a plan, and who sells it?

From inside the app. On Windows, Mac and Linux it opens Stripe's secure checkout in your browser; Stripe sells the plan through its Managed Payments service, shown on your receipt as Onelink. On Android you buy through Google Play. On iPhone and iPad you buy through the App Store, at Apple's own prices: £23.99 a year, £2.99 a month or £58.99 for Lifetime. A plan belongs to your Always Today account, so it works on every device you sign in on, wherever you bought it.

Can I get a refund?

Yes: refunds within 14 days, no questions asked, on top of your legal rights. The help page explains how, for the web, for Google Play and for the App Store.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your device. An account, just your email address with no password, is only needed to sync.

Can the sync server read my to-dos?

Not if you turn on end-to-end encryption: your devices encrypt every change before it leaves, with a passphrase only you know. Without it, your lists are stored on the server as written, protected in transit and at rest, and never read, sold or analysed.

Can I bring my lists from Things, Todoist or TickTick?

Yes. Always Today imports their exports and shows you what it found before adding anything. You can export everything as a JSON file at any time.
