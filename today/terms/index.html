What Always Today is

Always Today is a to-do list app for Android phones and tablets, Googlebooks, Windows and the Linux terminal, with an iPhone and iPad version planned. The apps are free to download and to use on one device.

The sync service is an optional online service that copies your lists between the devices you sign in on. Each device keeps its own full copy of your lists and works offline; our server passes changes between them. Sync comes with a paid plan, and with the free trial.

Your account

  • Keep your email account safe: anyone who can read your email can sign in. You can see your signed-in devices, and sign them out, in the app.
  • You must be at least 13 to create an account.
  • If you turn on end-to-end encryption, only your passphrase or your recovery key can unlock your synced lists. We can’t recover them if you lose both.
