The short version

The game collects nothing about you. There are no ads, no account, no analytics, no crash reports and no tracking. Things leave your phone only when you choose: a purchase of Full Rodeo goes to Google Play, and a video you share goes to the app you share it with. Neither comes to us.

What stays on your device

The game remembers a few settings so you don’t have to choose them again: sound, music and vibration, the players and controllers you picked, how good the bots are, the level and the points needed to win, and whether you have unlocked Full Rodeo. If you leave a match before it ends, it also keeps that match (the level, the round, the scores and the traps placed so far) so “Continue” can pick it up; it forgets the match when the match ends or after a week. All of this is kept in the app’s own storage on your device. We can’t see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Buying Full Rodeo

Four levels are free. Full Rodeo, a one-time purchase, unlocks nine more. The purchase is made through Google Play using Google’s billing library, which talks to the Google Play app on your device. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. Google tells the app only whether Full Rodeo was bought on your Google account, so it can unlock the levels and restore them on a new phone. Google’s own privacy policy covers what Google Play does with a purchase.

Because that library is built in, the app asks Android for internet access. The game itself sends nothing over the internet.

Sharing a moment

After a round, “Share this moment” makes a short video of the round’s best moment on your device and hands it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or Messages. The video goes only where you send it; it doesn’t come to us. Until you share another one, the latest video stays in the app’s cache on your device.

Links

Settings has links to the maker’s website (guzh.uk), this policy, Google Play and an email address. Tapping one opens your browser, Google Play or your email app, and from then on that service’s own privacy policy applies.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts to send any data, this page will say what, why and to whom before that version is released.
